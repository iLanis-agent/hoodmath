The cooktop

BTU is on the range spec sheet - add every burner at full power. Gas is sized by heat (BTU / 100), electric and induction by width (100 CFM per linear foot). Islands get 50% more because cross-drafts own the plume.

The duct run

Every elbow counts as 25 ft of duct, a cap as 15. Rigid metal only - flexible duct and its crushed cousin behind the stove are where CFM ratings go to die.

Your spec

Sizing only. Local code, the hood manufacturer's minimums, and a real installer outrank any web page. Downdrafts and recirculating "ductless" hoods are a different appliance with different (lower) expectations.

What the listing will not mention

The CFM on the box is in free airno duct attached, no elbows, no cap - a long real-world run can cost a third of the rated pull
400 CFM is a legal line, not a featureat or above it, most codes require makeup air - a ducted path for replacement air, or your house depressurizes and backdrafts the water heater
Sones matter more than CFM past 300a hood too loud to use is a 0 CFM hood; check the sone rating at the speed you will actually run
Capture area beats powera hood 3 in wider than the cooktop on each side at 450 CFM outperforms a narrow 900 CFM unit for everyday cooking
Duct size is not a suggestion600 CFM through a 6 in duct sounds like a jet and moves like a fan; match the duct to the math
Mount height is part of the spec24-30 in above the cooktop; every inch higher trades capture for headroom
